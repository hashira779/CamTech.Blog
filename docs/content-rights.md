# Content Rights & Media Licensing Policy

Section 36 Guidelines: Daily Discovery maintains strict compliance with digital copyright and intellectual property protections.

---

## 1. Allowed License Categories

1. **`OWNED`**: Original reporting, custom visual diagrams, and proprietary tools created by our newsroom.
2. **`LICENSED`**: Wire service subscriptions, commercial stock licenses with documented usage rights.
3. **`PUBLIC_DOMAIN`**: Government agency releases (e.g. NASA astronomical imagery, official ministerial public notices).
4. **`CC_LICENSE`**: Creative Commons assets utilized in accordance with attribution rules (e.g. CC-BY, CC-BY-SA).
5. **`EMBED_PERMITTED`**: Official YouTube, X, or social media embeds adhering to platform terms.

---

## 2. Image Attribution Rules

Every hero image and infographic displayed on Daily Discovery must include:
- Creator / Photographer name
- Licensing model
- Link to original publication or repository where applicable

If rights cannot be confirmed, the asset status remains `PENDING_REVIEW` and cannot be published alongside advertising slots.
